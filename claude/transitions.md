## Example from the lessons

Quiet Persuasion rewrites one health pitch so the problems cause each other. The book names no format. Jeremiah assigned this check `LONG / FLOW`.

Before: "Are you facing challenges with your child's dietary choices? Is your teen's fast-food habit worrying you? Are you overwhelmed by the conflicting diet advice for your child? We all want our kids to be healthy, but finding the right food balance is difficult and confusing."

After, as three linked questions in the book's rewrite:

"Is your teen's fast-food habit worrying you?"

"Are they gaining pounds of unattractive fat and seeing acne spots on their face?"

"Does this make them insecure and ashamed?"
