# Stories

Same checks as `CHECKLIST.md`. Day 28 is the source for every check below.

- [ ] Does the story have a recognizable person, a situation, a caused plot, stakes, and a resolution that serves the offer? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is the character flawed and specific, the want universal, and the want shown in a detail rather than a label like "wanted to lose weight"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL (stories) | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Are facts used when he is analytical or already aware, a short metaphor when the idea is complex, and a sensory scene only when there is room to be understood? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Does the long-form story show the ordinary world, the call to change, the resistance, the guide, and the transformation? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT} Caveat: and arc-splitting.
- [ ] Does the short-form story keep one moment, one emotion, and only the details that carry weight? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: SHORT | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Does the social post tell one core idea, in one post or split on purpose, short because he will be distracted? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: SHORT + THREAD | stage: QA}
- [ ] In the sales letter, does each story do one job — hook, mechanism, or proof — and stay a support for the solution? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: sales letter | stage: hook, explanation, or evidence}
- [ ] Is a result shown in a scene, not named with a label such as "she was nervous" or "our product is effective"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Does the story acknowledge his doubts, instead of skipping that beat? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] If the story is fictional, is it still rooted in something real, with flawed people and without an over-dramatic event? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL (stories) | stage: QA}

Level 1, facts. The lesson says this fits an analytical reader or one already aware. Jeremiah assigned this check `ALL / DRAFT`.

"Sarah Williams, a 34-year-old mother of two, struggled with weight loss for years. She lost 18 kilos in 6 months through a combination of our supplement and moderate exercise. Her blood pressure normalized, and she now maintains a healthy weight of 59 kilos."

Level 3, scene. The lesson says this needs room.

"The dressing room mirror wasn't just reflecting my image anymore - it was screaming at me. I stood there, surrounded by a pile of 'medium-sized' dresses that were all too small."
