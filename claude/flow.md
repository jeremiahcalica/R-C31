# Flow and cadence

Same checks as `CHECKLIST.md`.

- [ ] Are about 80% of sentences medium or short, about 20% long, and mixed so one length does not clump? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: FLOW}
- [ ] Do similar arguments sit in the same paragraph? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: FLOW}
- [ ] Does each sentence hold one idea, with a period or ellipsis instead of a comma between two breaths, and commas kept for lists? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: FLOW} Caveat: ellipsis use is client-voice dependent.
- [ ] Can it be read aloud without a stumble, and was any sentence that needs two breaths split? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: FLOW}
- [ ] Do the sales letter's first lines start in the action, and would cutting the first six sentences leave a stronger letter? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: sales letter | stage: first lines}
- [ ] Would cutting the liftnote's first six sentences make it punchier, and does a story in it start mid-action? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: liftnote | stage: opening}
- [ ] Are urgent moments in short sentences, and anticipation or calm in longer ones? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: story | stage: pace}
- [ ] Is the VSL punchy, with spoken pauses and fillers, a brisk unhurried tempo, and a pause after a key point? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: VSL | stage: delivery}
- [ ] Does the projected future name something a person can see or hold, not a blanket line like "we all want our kids to be healthy"? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Does the piece solve an immediate problem first, and only then name the identity change, as in "Product X solves immediate problem Y, and allows you to become person Z," rather than "Become Y person by buying product X"? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: FLOW}
- [ ] In the long-form sales letter, does the story's place come through sight, sound, smell, taste, or touch? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: long form sales letter | stage: ambience}
