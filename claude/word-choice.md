# Word choice

Same checks as `CHECKLIST.md`. The liftnote casual-and-contractions half is in `parked.md`. The Annex ban and the headline clichés are one check here.

- [ ] Is every word immediate, simple, and conversational, with a concrete picture instead of a fancy abstraction? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Are filler words gone wherever the meaning holds, and is the active voice used wherever it can be? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Does it stay short of a transcript of speech? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL (transcript posts) | stage: FLOW}
- [ ] Does the liftnote subject line use an action verb, and drop filler, adverbs, and "very"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: liftnote | stage: subject line}
- [ ] Does every feature survive a "so what?" that lands on his life, not on another feature? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Does the wording use his jargon, tone, and sentence shape, without parroting his lines? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT} Caveat: client voice from transcripts.
- [ ] Where the moment is uncertain, are "explosive" and "massive" absent, with "stable," "resilient," or "sustainable" in their place when a reassurance word is needed? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] If a headline uses a power word, does that word have one job, and is the stack thin? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: headline | stage: hook}
- [ ] If the headline is for a high-end product, does it use a status word such as Exclusive, Premium, or Elite? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: headline | stage: hook}
- [ ] Are these absent: "It is not your fault," "You are not alone," "I help busy ___," "100% money back guarantee," "Look at this image," "Just imagine," "We've got you covered," "This changes everything," "Supercharge," "Elevate," "Skyrocket," "Optimize," "Unleash," "Level up," "Take it to the next level," "In today's day and age," "Elite," "Exclusive," "Premium," "Revolutionary," "Skyrocket your success," "Unlock your potential," and "Elevate your brand"? [quiet-persuasion.md; copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}

The high-end status-word check and the merged ban list both stay, because both lessons state them. They conflict on Exclusive, Premium, and Elite. See the pull request.
