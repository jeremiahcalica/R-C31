# Claude layer

`CHECKLIST.md` is the file to upload to a Claude project. It is the ghostwriting list: 95 checks. The topic files repeat one group of those checks and add before/after lines taken from the lessons.

`parked.md` holds 10 sales-funnel checks. Leave it out of that upload.

Jeremiah assigned the tags the lessons had left open. This map is built from the tags now on the checks.

Vehicles: `ALL` every client post, `SHORT` a single X or LinkedIn post, `THREAD` a thread or listicle, `LONG` a long-form article. Qualifiers: `ALL (stories)`, `ALL (posts with a CTA)`, `ALL (transcript posts)`, `SHORT + THREAD`. Stages: `DRAFT` while writing, `FLOW` the flow edit, `HOOK`, `CLOSE`, `QA` the final pass. A vehicle the lesson already named (liftnote, sales letter, headline, and the rest) is listed under that name.

## While writing — stage DRAFT

- `pov-tense.md`: about the reader, his existing worldview, reader as hero, "them" as one person.
- `flow.md`: name something he can see or hold.
- `persuasion.md`: identity and how others see him, one big idea (one core idea per post), "different" made specific, staying put as the larger risk, a result treated as "when." On `LONG` only: prove the method, one bad cause, specific pillars with the private feeling left open, a fast win before the slower change, natural and mostly easy with one step he still does.
- `word-choice.md`: his jargon, tone, and sentence shape, from the transcript.
- `stories.md`: on `LONG`, the full story, the long-form arc (and arc-splitting), and the doubt beat. On `ALL (stories)`, a flawed specific character. On `ALL`, facts, metaphor, or a sensory scene matched to the reader. On `SHORT`, one moment and one emotion.

## Flow edit — stage FLOW

- `transitions.md`: each line follows (`ALL`); each story event causes the next (`ALL (stories)`); immediate, then short-term, then long-term (`LONG`); a question between sections connects them (`LONG`; watch the announcing-transitions ban).
- `flow.md`: 80/20 sentence length, one idea per sentence (ellipsis use is client-voice dependent), readable aloud (`ALL`); similar arguments in one paragraph, and problem Y before person Z (`LONG`).
- `persuasion.md`: pace the present, then one quiet lead (`LONG`).
- `word-choice.md`: short of a transcript of speech (`ALL (transcript posts)`).

## Hook — stage HOOK

- `hooks.md`: an opening story does not announce its purpose, and a pain opening is one trigger event (`ALL`). The same file holds the lesson-named hook checks below.
- `persuasion.md`: novelty is a twist on a familiar problem (`ALL`).
- `word-choice.md`: a headline power word has one job; a high-end headline may use Exclusive, Premium, or Elite. That status-word check still conflicts with the ban list under QA.

## Close — stage CLOSE

- `closes.md`: when the post has a CTA, the action is in small explicit steps, and urgency is a real limit. The same file holds the lesson-named close checks below.

## Final pass — stage QA

- `pov-tense.md`: a fresh reader can name speaker, audience, problem, solution, and promise.
- `persuasion.md`: a new cause is shown and "It is not your fault" stays unsaid; past results match this reader; the promise could be said to his mother.
- `word-choice.md`: immediate simple concrete words, filler cut and active voice, every feature survives "so what?", "explosive" and "massive" dropped in an uncertain moment, and the merged ban list (Annex phrases plus "Skyrocket your success," "Unlock your potential," "Elevate your brand").
- `stories.md`: one core idea (`SHORT + THREAD`); the result is shown, not labeled (`ALL`); a fictional story stays rooted in reality (`ALL (stories)`).
