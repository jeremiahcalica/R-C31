# Persuasion

Quiet-persuasion checks, plus the sales lessons that state the same principles. Same wording as `CHECKLIST.md`. The USP check is in `parked.md`.

- [ ] Does he feel a change in identity and in how others see him, with a logical reason available after that? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is there one big idea, and is it unique, relevant, simple, emotionally appealing, and memorable? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT} Caveat: one core idea per post.
- [ ] Does the letter make "different" specific enough to explain why it works, instead of stopping at "better"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Does it prove this way of solving the problem, instead of competing on specs, features, or price? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is there one decisively bad cause that only this mechanism undoes, and is the reader not that villain? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] If blame leaves the reader, does the draft show a new cause, with the sentence "It is not your fault" left unsaid? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Does he reach the conclusion himself, with no "you should want this"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: sales letter | stage: comparison}
- [ ] Are who, problem, desire, solution, mechanism, outcome, and cost specific, while his private feeling of the new life stays open unless known? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is a fast measurable win stated before the slower change, and is that slower change still said to be underway? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is the novelty a twist on a familiar problem, not a claim of total strangeness? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: HOOK}
- [ ] Is staying put the larger risk, and does a clear path answer the specific fears of acting? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Does the process feel natural and mostly easy, with one step he still does if total ease would feel like cheating? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Does a casual line treat the result as normal, so the question is "when," not "if"? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Do past results match this reader's situation, rather than a famous name alone? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Could the writer say this promise to his own mother about this problem? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] When he has to see what the product does, does the line follow "[Product] does [benefit] thanks to [feature]," rather than a benefit with the feature missing? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: welcome sequence | stage: email 2}
- [ ] Does the benefit land on a changed relationship, so he can see himself in it? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: offer section | stage: offer}
- [ ] If the draft paces the present moment, do several things that are true now come before one quiet lead? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: FLOW}

## Examples from the lessons

Show the cause. Do not say the banned sentence. Quiet Persuasion calls this a persuasion attempt and names no stage. Jeremiah assigned this check `ALL / QA`.

Before: "It is not your fault."

After: "You have done your best in losing weight, but the food industry will simply not let you achieve your goals. They profit off keeping Americans fat."

Source: [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}

A casual line that assumes the result. Jeremiah assigned this check `ALL / DRAFT`.

"I cannot be held responsible for the new clothes you will have to buy."

"Be aware that your hairdresser budget may increase in the next few months of using this product."

Source: [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}

Mother-test pair from Day 11. The lesson names no vehicle. The spoken line is the one it rejects for a cancer product. Jeremiah assigned this check `ALL / QA`.

Rejected: "Hey, Mom, I found an incredible method that comes from China that will cure your cancer in 4 weeks."

Kept direction: "Follow your chemo and radiotherapy but suffer less from the consequences of your treatment."

Source: [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}

Quoted, then rejected: Don't say "5GB of storage..." Say "1,000 songs in your pocket."
