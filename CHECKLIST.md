# Draft checklist

Yes/no checks for a client post, merged where lessons overlap. No rule was added from outside the two source files. Jeremiah assigned the tags the lessons had left open.

`ALL` is every client post. `SHORT` is a single X or LinkedIn post. `THREAD` is a thread or listicle. `LONG` is a long-form article. Stages are `DRAFT` (while writing), `FLOW` (flow edit), `HOOK`, `CLOSE`, and `QA` (final pass before delivery). A tag the lesson already named, such as liftnote, sales letter, or headline, stays in the lesson's words.

Qualifiers travel with the check: `ALL (stories)`, `ALL (posts with a CTA)`, `ALL (transcript posts)`, and `SHORT + THREAD`. A caveat on a line is a limit on that check. It is not a new rule.

Sales-funnel-only checks live in `parked.md`, outside this list. Upload this file to a Claude project. Topic files repeat one group of these checks and add quoted before/after pairs.

- [ ] Is there one big idea, and is it unique, relevant, simple, emotionally appealing, and memorable? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT} Caveat: one core idea per post.

- [ ] Does he reach the conclusion himself, with no "you should want this"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: sales letter | stage: comparison}
- [ ] Are who, problem, desire, solution, mechanism, outcome, and cost specific, while his private feeling of the new life stays open unless known? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is a fast measurable win stated before the slower change, and is that slower change still said to be underway? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: LONG | stage: DRAFT}
- [ ] Is the novelty a twist on a familiar problem, not a claim of total strangeness? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: HOOK}

- [ ] Does a casual line treat the result as normal, so the question is "when," not "if"? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT}

## Word choice

- [ ] Is every word immediate, simple, and conversational, with a concrete picture instead of a fancy abstraction? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Are filler words gone wherever the meaning holds, and is the active voice used wherever it can be? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Does it stay short of a transcript of speech? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL (transcript posts) | stage: FLOW}
- [ ] Does the liftnote subject line use an action verb, and drop filler, adverbs, and "very"? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: liftnote | stage: subject line}
- [ ] Does every feature survive a "so what?" that lands on his life, not on another feature? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] Does the wording use his jargon, tone, and sentence shape, without parroting his lines? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: DRAFT} Caveat: client voice from transcripts.
- [ ] Where the moment is uncertain, are "explosive" and "massive" absent, with "stable," "resilient," or "sustainable" in their place when a reassurance word is needed? [quiet-persuasion.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}
- [ ] If a headline uses a power word, does that word have one job, and is the stack thin? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: headline | stage: hook}
- [ ] If the headline is for a high-end product, does it use a status word such as Exclusive, Premium, or Elite? [copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: headline | stage: hook}
- [ ] Are these absent: "It is not your fault," "You are not alone," "I help busy ___," "100% money back guarantee," "Look at this image," "Just imagine," "We've got you covered," "This changes everything," "Supercharge," "Elevate," "Skyrocket," "Optimize," "Unleash," "Level up," "Take it to the next level," "In today's day and age," "Elite," "Exclusive," "Premium," "Revolutionary," "Skyrocket your success," "Unlock your potential," and "Elevate your brand"? [quiet-persuasion.md; copywriting-lessons-days-2-28.md] {vehicle: ALL | stage: QA}

The high-end status-word check and the merged ban list both stay, because both lessons state them. They conflict on Exclusive, Premium, and Elite.
